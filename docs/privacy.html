Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Cube Tower (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by an individual developer under the name "jic51" ("we", "us", "our", or "the developer of AR Cube Tower"). We wrote this policy in plain language so it is easy to understand what happens with your information.

The short version

  • The camera is used only to power augmented reality on your device. Camera images are never recorded, saved or sent to any server.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your display name, avatar, progress, coins, gems, lives and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or location.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • You can erase all game data at any time by uninstalling the App.

1. Camera and augmented reality

AR Cube Tower is an augmented reality game. It needs your camera to find a flat surface, such as a table or the floor, and to show the virtual platform and cubes on top of the real world.

  • Camera processing happens entirely on your device, in real time.
  • We do not record video, take photos or save camera images.
  • Camera images are never sent to us or to any server.

Android will ask for your permission before the App can use the camera. If you decline, the augmented reality gameplay cannot work, but you can change your choice later in your device settings.

2. Information stored on your device

The App does not have accounts, logins or registration. To remember your game between sessions, it saves the following information locally on your phone, in a local file and in the device's standard app preferences:

InformationWhy it is stored
Display name you choose (optional) and avatarTo show your name and avatar inside the game
Level progressSo you can continue where you left off
Coins, gems and livesTo keep track of your in-game balance
Settings (sound, vibration)To remember your preferences

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it. Please do not use your real full name or other sensitive information as your display name.

3. What we do not collect

The App does not use or collect:

  • Analytics or usage tracking tools
  • Crash reporting services
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Your location
  • Your microphone
  • Your contacts
  • Files, photos or other content from your device storage

4. Android permissions

The App asks for only two permissions:

  • Camera — used only for augmented reality, as described above.
  • Internet — used only for the App's ad system and to open links you choose to tap.

Whenever any app connects to the internet, the servers it reaches can see basic technical details that come with every connection, such as an IP address. We do not use this to identify you, build a profile or track you.

5. Ads and links to other websites

The App shows its own augmented reality ad breaks ("AR ad breaks"). You may choose to watch one to get rescued after losing a level, and an ad break may also appear from time to time after you lose. These ads come from the App's own ad system and do not use advertising identifiers or tracking.

Some ads include a button that opens the advertiser's website in your phone's web browser. That only happens if you tap the button. Once you leave the App, the advertiser's website is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other websites, so please review their policies.

6. Sharing and selling

Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, we have nothing to sell or share. We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you remove it. Uninstalling the App from your phone deletes all of its data. You can also clear it without uninstalling from your phone's system settings (Settings → Apps → AR Cube Tower → Storage → Clear storage).

Since we do not store your data on any server, there is nothing to delete on our side. If you still have questions or requests about your data, you are welcome to email us at joseisrael5101@gmail.com.

8. Security

Keeping data on your device, instead of on remote servers, greatly reduces the risk of it being exposed. Your game data is protected by the same security that protects other apps on your phone, so we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we have designed the App to collect as little as possible.

9. Children's privacy (COPPA)

AR Cube Tower is intended for people aged 13 and older and is rated Teen. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at joseisrael5101@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it. You can also remove all game data from the device by uninstalling the App.

10. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you rights over your personal information, including the right to know what is collected, to request deletion, to request correction, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. You also have the right not to be treated differently for exercising these rights.

In practice:

  • We do not collect personal information on our servers.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we have not done so in the past 12 months.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information.

If you want to ask about any of these rights, email us at joseisrael5101@gmail.com.

11. Users in the EEA and UK (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR and UK GDPR) gives you rights over your personal data. These include the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off camera access in your device settings; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because the App keeps your game data only on your own device, you already have full control over it: you can change your display name and avatar in the App, and erase everything by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Camera use relies on the permission you grant, and local storage of your game data is needed to provide the game you asked to play. For any request, contact us at joseisrael5101@gmail.com.

12. Google Play

The App will be distributed through Google Play. When you download or update apps there, Google handles information according to its own privacy policy. We do not control Google's data practices.

When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change significantly affects how your information is handled, we will make reasonable efforts to let you know, for example through a notice in the App. Please check this page from time to time.
