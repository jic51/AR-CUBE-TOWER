How to play

Scan. Place. Stack.

Every level happens right in front of you, on a real surface in your room.

  1. Scan

    Point your camera at a flat surface such as a table or the floor and move it slowly until the game detects it.

  2. Place

    Put down your virtual platform. Pinch to scale it, twist to rotate it, then anchor it in place.

  3. Stack

    A crane delivers cubes one by one. Aim with the crosshair, release, and build up to the goal height before time runs out.

Watch your balance: if a cube lands with half of it hanging in the air, it falls.

11 cube types

Not every cube plays nice

Each material behaves differently. Learn how they interact and plan every drop.

  • Normal

    The reliable classic. Solid and predictable.

  • Lead

    Very heavy. It can crush what sits below it.

  • Feather

    So light it bounces. Fire burns it away.

  • Ice

    Slippery surface. Melts when it meets fire.

  • Fire

    Explosive. Burns feathers and melts ice.

  • Jelly

    Soft and wobbly. Handle with care.

  • Lava

    A molten block with a temper.

  • Grass

    A chunk of earth with a green top.

  • Water

    A block of water that keeps things interesting.

  • Cloud

    Soft and airy, straight from the sky.

  • Stone

    Rough, sturdy and dependable.

Coins, gems and lives

Earn coins by playing and gems from special rewards. You have up to 5 lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes. Coins and gems are in-game currency only, with no real-world value. There are currently no real-money purchases.

Privacy & safety

Your room stays yours

AR Cube Tower was built to keep things simple and private.

Read the Privacy Policy Read the Terms of Use
